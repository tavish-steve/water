Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Water Marc Builders a licensed contractor in Kenya?

Yes. We're registered with the National Construction Authority (NCA5), with our license valid through 2028 — you can verify our standing before you hire us.

Do you charge for an initial site visit or quote?

No. Our first site visit and project quote are completely free, with no obligation to proceed.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve anywhere and are not limited by location. While we commonly work in Nairobi and its environs — including Karen, Langata, South C, Thika Road, Ruiru, Kiambu, Runda, Kitisuru, Juja and Kamakis — we are happy to take on projects across broader locations as well.

Can you handle both design and construction?

Yes — our design-build service covers architectural design, structural and MEP engineering, and construction, so one team is accountable from concept to handover.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope and size. During your free site visit we'll give you a realistic schedule based on your specific project and current site conditions.

Do you offer staged or milestone-based payments?

Yes. We structure payments around project milestones so you only pay for work completed and verified — ask us about this during your quote.
